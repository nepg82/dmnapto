      Check against a paystub

      Enter the balances from a paystub and the planner compares them with its own numbers for that date. A mismatch usually means a day taken is missing, or a rule differs from what HR applies.

      Git sync

      Saves your time-off entries to a JSON file in a GitHub repo. Use a fine-grained token limited to that one repo with Contents: read and write. The token is stored only in this browser and is never written to the data file. A private repo is best, since the file lists your days off.

      

      


      Rules used

        

      • Everything is computed from your hire date, June 4, 2026, with 40 h of Personal at hire, plus the time you enter. Pay periods are 14 days and accruals post on each period's last day.
        • 

      • Each pay period: +4 h Vacation and +4 h Sick (Vacation becomes 6 h from the 8th anniversary).
        • 

      • Vacation can't be used until 13 pay periods are complete (Dec 3, 2026). Everything accrued before then counts once unlocked.
        • 

      • Each June 4: Personal resets to 40 h (the old balance is lost). Vacation is capped at 320 h first, then +8 h × years of service through the 7th anniversary.
        • 

      • Sick is capped at 2,000 h.
        • 

      • Floaters: Lincoln's Birthday (Feb 12) and Election Day (Tuesday after the first Monday in November), +8 h each, expiring on the next occurrence.
        • 

      • Holiday: +8 h when New Year's Day, July 4, Veterans Day or Christmas lands on a Saturday. It expires one year after it is earned.
        • 

      • Time is used oldest-expiring first. Assumptions to confirm with HR: the vacation cap date, the 8th-anniversary change, and the sick cap.
